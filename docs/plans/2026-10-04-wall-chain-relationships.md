# Whole-wall relationships across point insertion

Splitting a wall must preserve its identity as one relationship driver. Store an
ordered list of physical wall members on the logical reference, rewrite that
list on every further split, and move the complete span and its dependents as
one reversible authored operation. Existing singleton references retain their
schema-one representation. Explicit chains use schema two, retained through
sync, Undo and project history.

The model worker owns the relationship codec, pure geometry resolver, sealed
dependent command adapter, wall-split rewrite and focused core fixtures. The
reader worker owns document admission, native/exchange format floors and opaque
future-model recovery. Root owns desktop composition, format-version header,
CMake, integration, native execution, delivery and Git. The read-only advisor
reviews physical identity, proof replay, cross-record aliases and retained
history. All native writers freeze during every native build and execution.

Capture baseline failures before implementation. Verify straight and curved
spans, recursive splitting, strict ownership and geometry admission, unchanged
ordinary schema-one output, future-model read-only preservation, active/undone/
deleted history and downgraded marker refusal. Exercise actual desktop sync,
preview, cancel, apply, all member movement, dependent geometry, Undo/Redo and
save/reopen. Review the candidate and captures before packaging and installation.

This closes a specific accepted requirement. It does not certify the full
production release, Apex compatibility or normative ANSI compliance.
