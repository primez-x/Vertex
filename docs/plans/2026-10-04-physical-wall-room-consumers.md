# Source-bound physical rooms

This checkpoint connects the Pinc comparison's wall-defined room classification
gap to usable Vertex authoring. It does not close the unified production gate.

## Outcome and ownership

- Geometry worker: strict OCCT-free descriptor codec, immutable canonical room
  query, identified room creation, bounded source validation and core fixtures.
- Desktop verification worker: actual room creation, fill/picking, labels,
  Details, schedule, persistence, Undo and exterior GLA regression fixture.
- Root: document/history fences, native/extraction reader floors, desktop
  palette/creation/scene/Details, schedule and CAD consumers, CMake, integration,
  native jobs, source kit and Git delivery.
- Independent advisor: read-only architecture and integrated source review.

## Contract

Use identified `room_boundary` owners, never independent appraisal measured
areas. Reserve `extensions.physical_wall_room` version 1 for selected source wall,
full detector lineage and exact analytical hole loops. No persisted claimed area
authorizes a quantity. Rebuild complete context/elevation/semantic phase and require
exact outer, holes and lineage agreement before reporting a current room value.
Presentation visibility does not alter geometry. Classification creates no facts.

Ordinary edits cannot detach provenance, replace holes or independently move the
room outline. Physical source edits leave the retained room stale. Current scene,
quantity, schedule and export consumers use one query; stale quantities and fill
are withheld with diagnostics. Source-bound Entity-only dimensions and outline
relationship propagation are refused until their snapshot-aware implementation.
Native format 43 and extraction 41 protect semantics in every retained revision.

## Verification and remaining work

Build the desktop application and affected core/desktop regression targets while
all writers are frozen. Verify clear area with an isolated wall hole, exact fill
and picking, classification, unchanged exterior GLA, save/reopen, Undo/Redo,
source insertion/removal/thickness changes, future version handling, DXF/IFC
loops and stale suppression. Inspect captured light/dark native UI.

Same-ID source repair, proven split/merge correspondence, complete room preset
mapping and source-aware editable room annotations remain required follow-up
work. Historical centerline rooms are not silently migrated. The full Apex/Pinc
inventory, compatibility and production acceptance requirements remain active.
