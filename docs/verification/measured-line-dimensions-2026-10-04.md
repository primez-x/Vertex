through the existing creator and canvas context action. Stable final edges and
revisited vertices resolve from receipt-backed replay. The angle picker offers
actual shared points for distinct chosen edges. Stroke area dimensions remain
unavailable, even when the stroke closes; defined appraisal areas remain the
source of GLA. Future models stay opaque/read-only and malformed known references
refuse atomically.

Ordinary edits recalculate values while retaining saved text positions and
presentation. Typed measured transforms and mixed typed boundary groups
reconstruct positions once with the complete pivot, reflection, rotation and
offset. Ordinary mixed-object movement proves each measured payload against
source replay, transforms its owned dimensions and validates the complete
transaction. A subset solver cannot reject a valid group whose selected anchor
also moves. The real Transform selection dialog previews strokes and labels,
supports Cancel/Apply and creates independent transformed copies.

Copy/Paste remaps owner, edge and shared-point identities. Pasted dimensions are
assigned to the destination drawing context instead of retaining missing source
building references. Cut/Delete collects owned dimensions and is reversible.
The same analytical projection supplies native canvases, SVG and PDF. Automatic
placement uses the active workspace's zoom rather than the other canvas's zoom.

Native 36 and extraction 34 protect dimension-bearing measured owners across
every retained revision, including genuine v1 strokes, entity-only files and
undone/deleted dimensions. Both native markers and the public format constant
agree. No dimension schema or SQLite table change is required. Marker downgrade
refusal preserves file bytes. The project-package validator accepts 36 and
continues to reject the next unsupported format.
