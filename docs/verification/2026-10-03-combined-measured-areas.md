# Combined measured areas

The measured-area review now combines selected adjoining new regions into one
classified analytical boundary. Shared dividers remain editable measured lines
and are excluded from the area's exterior perimeter. The preview shows the
combined net once. Cancel changes nothing; Apply is one history operation.

The baseline is the official Apex v7 Draw First tutorial, pages 8–9:
https://apexwin.com/support/ApexSketchv7/ApexSketchv7-DrawFirst.pdf.
The saved reference and actual native evidence are under the local
`artifacts/combined-measured-areas-20261003` directory. They are local evidence,
not bundled normative or compatibility certification.

## Verified behavior

- Straight, concave and curved connected unions retain exact analytical edges.
  Disjoint, point-only, nested-overlap and hole-producing selections are refused.
- Complete member lineage includes cancelled internal seams. Missing, hidden,
  ambiguous or changed topology withholds dependent totals. A unique unchanged
  topology can refresh geometry and all source metadata together.
- Existing groups retain membership, classification and identity on re-detection.
  Partial reuse and implicit consumption of existing definitions are refused.
- Two- and four-region desktop previews support shared classification, Separate,
  Cancel, Apply, Details GLA, save/reopen and exact Undo/Redo.
- Clone, movement and clipboard paste retain and remap every member source.
  Pasted measured graphs appear beside existing geometry, remain selected, and
  retain source-driven editing. Editing a copy leaves the original unchanged.
- An actual disconnected divider leaves the saved group unchanged and stale,
  with qualified GLA withheld. Undo restores the complete operation.
- Native format 33 and extraction 31 retain group metadata through history;
  forged reader-floor downgrades are refused. Unknown group schemas are preserved
  read-only. Unrelated vendor fields remain opaque and editable.
- Light and dark native captures have readable classification labels and text.
  Source matching has explicit allocation and work budgets.
