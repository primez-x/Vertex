# Curved survey calls and retained-source protection

The confirmed straight-only survey authoring gap is filled with chord/angle,
chord/height and chord/arc-length calls. Calculation, source reopening, analytical
preview, insertion and correction share one parser/rebuilder. A sealed rebuilt
report verifies entered calls and receipts before producing geometry; stored
vertices and diagnostics are regenerated. Straight-only reports retain their v1
schema and numerical path. Curved reports use v2 and preserve exact quantity
receipts, direction and construction parameters.

Measured perimeter excludes proposed closure geometry. Closure never silently
moves an arc endpoint. A valid straight-final endpoint adjustment requires an
explicit choice; Add/Update remain blocked while the selected proposal is
invalid. An uninsertable closing segment preserves measured area, preview and
report export. Curved final calls cannot use endpoint adjustment.

Source wrappers retain v2 after later straight corrections. Native34/exchange32
protection scans all retained revisions, including Undo and deleted owners.
Future current/original report and input metadata is retained with read-only
protection. Structural admission does not certify archival receipt accuracy;
consumption requires sealed reconstruction. Legitimate live drawing edits may
differ from recorded calls without being rejected as corrupt geometry.
