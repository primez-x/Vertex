# Independent wall measurement presentation

Close the automatic-wall portion of U132–U135/APX-ANNO-002: place a wall's
measurement independently, edit paper text height/color/emphasis/rotation,
hide and restore it, and retain those choices through history and save/reopen.
The number remains derived from the analytical wall baseline. Measurement
presentation never changes or hides the wall, its openings, or appraisal facts.

Use annotation v6 `wall_dimension` presentation records keyed by wall ID.
Manual positions are world XY offsets from the analytical midpoint. Automatic
placement uses the current exterior side and can be restored independently of
style or visibility. Placement-only records inherit automatic theme appearance.
Named plans transform the text anchor, leader, rotation and automatic baseline;
pointer placement is inverse-projected. Shared derivation serves geometry
previews, committed canvas and output.

The wall's contextual Dimension group provides style/visibility and
Place measurement/Automatic controls. A Wall measurement context command
exposes the same controls even when the measurement is hidden. Existing
boundary dimensions and annotation coordinate modes retain their behavior.
