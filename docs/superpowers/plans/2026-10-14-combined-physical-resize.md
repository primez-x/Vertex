# Combined physical resize implementation plan

> For agentic workers: use the existing delegated delivery workflow and
> superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans as
> applicable. User authorization and repository ownership rules remain binding.

**Goal:** Resize independent components together with at least two qualified
physical families through one source-bound side-grip gesture and one Undo.

**Architecture:** Independently derive physical contributions from the original
snapshot, union at most three through existing typed carrier mergers, then run
the existing two-candidate component/dimension finalizer once. Keep historical
Properties39/40/41 and their bounds unchanged.

**Tech stack:** C++20, Qt, existing geometry/document authoring and SQLite stores.

**Spec:** [Combined physical side resizing](../../design/vertex-combined-physical-resize.md).

## Global constraints

- Closed Properties42/command52; reader265/extraction263.
- Nonempty component geometry request7/8/9 and at least two physical family lists.
- One aggregate 1000-member bound and one aggregate 1000-association bound.
- Distinct private catalog copies retain qualified root destinations and proofs.
- No completed-command splicing or global increase of the retirement-aware bound.
- Root owns native jobs and Git; no edits to consumed inputs during a job.

## Review focus

- Shared baseline catalogs: preserve original rows and distinct qualified copies.
- Units with unsupported references: refuse before changed-owner elimination.
- Cross-family fresh aliases: retain global-ID and qualified-token distinctions.
- Review/history-only staleness: reject without source or committed-output changes.
- Opaque command-shaped metadata: keep reader floors tied to canonical proof slots.
