# Appraisal measurements from closing a wall outline

Outcome: in an explicitly enabled Appraisal workflow with an Exterior measurement
basis, closing an interactive wall outline creates its exact exterior measurement
and dimensions automatically. The physical walls and measured boundary remain
separate objects with recorded source relationships. The closing wall, its
connections and measurement share one reversible document operation.

The Details tab remains the place for GLA, category totals and area dimensions.
Automatic geometry creation does not declare grade, finish, access, ceiling or
ANSI observations. Missing facts continue to withhold qualified totals. Vertex
does not claim ANSI approval or certification.
